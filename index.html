FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do I have to use both apps?

No. KaiaMalha ships with default building templates and you can draw an entire city without ever opening KaiaDNA. KaiaDNA is for those who want to author custom building rules.

Which export formats are supported?

KaiaMalha exports in a universal format accepted by Blender, Unreal Engine and Maya. Materials and geometry are preserved.

Can I run on integrated GPU?

Yes. Intel Iris Xe, AMD Radeon 680M and Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) handle KaiaDNA and small KaiaMalha scenes well. A discrete GPU is recommended for large cities.

My export file is huge. What do I do?

Reduce tree density in Inspector → 3D View (Trees: Sparse or Off). You can also disable the traffic-lights option if you don't need it.

Is Linux supported?

Technically yes, but it's not officially validated yet. Linux bug reports are welcome.
